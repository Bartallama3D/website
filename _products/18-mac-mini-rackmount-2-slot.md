The Bartallama3D™ 2U Rackmount for the Mac mini M4, M5 Pro, and M6 (2-slot) mounts two Mac minis in a standard 19" server rack. The Mac mini draws its intake air from the front underside of the chassis and places its power button on the rear, so this rackmount was designed around both: a vent supplies fresh air to the Mac mini from the front of the server rack, and an actuator brings the power button to the front. The faceplate is steel rather than plastic, which keeps the rackmount rigid.

Key Features:

- Rigid steel faceplate spanning the full 19" width in 2U. It will not bend or deflect under the weight of the Mac minis.
- Power button on the front panel provides easy access to boot or force-restart the Mac minis without reaching around to the rear of the rack.
- Front vents supply fresh air from the front of the rack to the Mac mini's underside intake. Without them, the exhaust leaving the rear of the Mac mini forms a pocket of hot air that gets recirculated straight back through the intake.
- Two keystone slots per Mac mini. One in each pair comes pre-populated with a Cat6 insert and Cat6 patch cable for the Mac mini's rear Ethernet port, and the other is fitted with a blank.
- Ships fully assembled. Install the Mac minis in the rackmount first, then mount the assembly in the rack.

This listing includes the rackmount, two Cat6 keystone inserts with patch cables, two keystone blanks, the hardware to install both Mac minis, and the rack hardware (cage nuts and screws). A Phillips screwdriver is required for rack installation and is not included.
