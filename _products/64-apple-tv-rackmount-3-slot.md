The Bartallama3D™ 1U Rackmount for the Apple TV 4K 3rd Generation (3-slot) mounts three Apple TV 4K units in a standard 19" server rack. Each unit can be installed facing forward or facing rearward, so the ports can be presented at the front of the rack alongside your patch panels rather than routed around the back. The faceplate is steel rather than plastic, which keeps the rackmount rigid across the full 19" span in a single U.

Key Features:

- Rigid steel faceplate spanning the full 19" width in 1U. It will not bend or deflect under the weight of the Apple TVs.
- Reversible mounting. Install each Apple TV facing forward for a clean front panel, or facing rearward to bring power, HDMI, and Ethernet (on Ethernet-equipped models) to the front of the rack.
- Two screws per Apple TV. Each unit drops into its holder and secures with a pair of screws, and all mounting hardware is included.
- Ships fully assembled. Install the Apple TVs in the rackmount first, then mount the assembly in the rack.

This listing includes the rackmount, the hardware to install three Apple TVs, and the rack hardware (four screws and cage nuts). A Phillips screwdriver is required for rack installation and is not included.
